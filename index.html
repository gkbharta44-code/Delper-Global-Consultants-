Executive Summary · Revised October 2026

Delper Group

A dual-track digital commerce and security group. From Year 1 GK Bharta holds 100% of the group, funded by palm oil income, with two trading platforms and the Delper Aegis launch in Year 7.

Seed Round: USD 8.00M from Investor

Seed investors put USD 8.00 Million into the two trading platforms, USD 4.00 Million in each. In Year 1 GK Bharta pays them USD 8.00 Million, which returns their principal and buys back their 40% interest. GK funds that payment from palm oil income of about USD 8.00 Million a year. In Year 1 GK also acquires Radiant Synage's 60% interest in the group for USD 4.00 Million. From Year 1 GK holds 100% of the group. In Year 7 GK funds the USD 1.90 Billion Delper Aegis launch and owns 100% of it.

GK Ownership Transition

From Year 1, GK Bharta holds 100% of Delper Group. It acquires the seed investors' 40% for USD 8.00 Million and Radiant Synage's 60% for USD 4.00 Million. Radiant's USD 12.00 Million contribution of intellectual property, banking lines and infrastructure is now an intra-group asset and is not treated as an external valuation.

  • Delper Group: GK Bharta 100% from Year 1.
  • Track A (Delper Global Consultants, Singapore SPV): 60% of after-tax profit is reinvested in escrow; 40% goes to GK Bharta.
  • Track B (Delper Retail, India): 60% of after-tax profit is reinvested in escrow; 40% goes to GK Bharta.
  • Delper Aegis: GK Bharta 100%. GK funds the full launch and receives 100% of its after-tax profit.

Dual-Track Operational Architecture

Track A: Delper Global Consultants (Singapore SPV)

  • Focus: Cross-border B2B bulk enterprise trade platform utilizing Radiant's trust-layer integration model.
  • Initial capital (USD 4.0M, seed investors): USD 2.21M trading capital and USD 1.79M set-up, including SAP BTP and Signavio middleware ($0.86M), SAP S/4HANA Core ERP ($0.39M), and SGS quality webhooks ($0.15M).
  • Unit Economics: 15.184% take rate on premium commodities and 24.2% on consumer and high-margin goods, over a 14-day settlement cycle (26 capital turns per year from Year 2; 17.6 in Year 1).
  • Profit: net profit is 84.15% of revenue after 17% tax and OpEx. 60% is reinvested in escrow and 40% goes to GK Bharta.

Track B: Delper Retail (India Domestic Engine)

  • Focus: India-focused, buyer-first digital marketplace integrated with the Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC).
  • Initial capital (USD 4.0M, seed investors): spent on the Year 1 pilot, covering core team and engineering, 3PQC set-up, escrow and licensing, RTB vetting, contingency and cloud.
  • Unit Economics: 2% commission on each sale, deducted from escrow release. Sellers pay a USD 100 annual onboarding fee and prepay real-time bidding and 3PQC inspection costs at actual cost. Buyers pay no separate fee.
  • Profit: net profit is 41.5% of gross profit after 50% expenses and 17% tax. 60% is reinvested in escrow and 40% goes to GK Bharta.
  • Five-Year Target: 7.5M orders and buyers and 15,000 certified sellers in Year 5, India only through ONDC.

Seed Round Buyback by GK, Funded by Palm Oil

The palm oil vehicle routes 800,000 metric tons of bulk palm oil a year through irrevocable at-sight letters of credit. GK Bharta must earn about USD 10.00 Million a year from palm oil to fund the buyback and the Radiant payment in Year 1. At 800,000 tons the USD 10 per ton fee gives only USD 8.00 Million, so the volume must rise to 1,000,000 tons, or the fee must reach USD 12.50 per ton. In Year 1 GK uses this income to pay the seed investors USD 8.00 Million, which returns their principal and buys back their 40% interest. The palm oil business continues as usual after the buyback. Malaysia is used only for palm oil purchasing, to clear the debt.

Delper Aegis: Factory-as-a-Service, 100% Capital from GK

Delper Aegis is an industrial-grade cybersecurity layer built around the Delper Guard firewall, with an initial hardware run of 3,000,000 units. GK Bharta funds its USD 1.90 Billion launch in Year 7, covering USD 531.5 Million of silicon development and USD 1.37 Billion of hardware. Revenue starts in Year 7.

  • Certification: IEC 62443 SL-4 is a target, not a rating. Formal testing is scheduled for Years 1 to 2.
  • Recurring revenue: USD 188.5 Billion a year at 1,000,000 lines on the revised bill of USD 15,706.50 a month per line. If customers accept only the USD 8,371.50 source subscription, this falls to about USD 100.5 Billion.
  • Workforce: the 12-line planning basis needs about 350,000 field engineers, against a talent model supporting about 159,390. Hiring capacity is the binding constraint.
  • Verdict: conditionally viable. It requires plants averaging about 26 or more lines, customers accepting a total bill near USD 15,707 per line per month, and full funding of the launch.

Reconciliation Points

These items are not yet supported by the source documents, or need confirmation before external distribution.

  • Palm oil income: confirmed at USD 10.00M every year. The source's 800,000 ton, USD 10 per ton fee gives USD 8.00M, so volume must rise to 1,000,000 tons, or the fee to USD 12.50 per ton. The source does not yet show how this will be achieved.
  • Buyback: the USD 8.00M covers both the seed investors' principal and their 40% interest. Confirm the price and the legal documents for each track.
  • Seed investors: names are not in the source documents, so the summary refers to them as seed investors.
  • Radiant interest: confirm that the 60% is Radiant Synage's interest in Delper Group, and document the USD 4.00M purchase and its legal structure.
  • Track A take rate: the Trust summary applies 24.2% to consumer goods and 15.184% to premium commodities. Confirm which rate applies to the B2B bulk platform.
  • Track A growth: revenue depends on 26 capital turns a year from Year 2. The Trust document does not yet evidence this.
  • Track B after Year 5: Years 6 to 10 use the 1-billion-buyer scenario from the Retail file.
  • Aegis: SL-4 is a target. The 8,900 qualified-engineer figure is unverified. Aegis revenue uses the supply-limited Scenario B.
  • Trust and Retail documents: they still show the earlier 25% and 40% structures, and the Trust document still shows a USD 12.0M pre-money valuation. Both need updating.
