Delper Group: Executive Summary

Dual-track digital architecture, investor exit pipeline, and long-term scaling plan

Delper Group is deploying a dual-track digital architecture that combines Delper Global Consultants (a cross-border B2B trade platform) and Delper Retail (an India-focused, buyer-first e-commerce marketplace via ONDC). Both platforms build their core operations as planned. In parallel, the company will use a high-volume bulk palm oil trade engine as a dedicated financial exit vehicle: routing 800,000 metric tons annually through Irrevocable At-Sight Letters of Credit (LCs) generates $8.0M in gross logistics fees, enough to repay both seed investors in a single lump-sum payout within 12 months, without early equity dilution or debt drag.

Core Business Units

Delper Global Consultants

Core process: Onboards trading parties through a unified API, running a 14-day settlement cycle (26 turns per year from Year 2).

Logistics and customs: Goods move through a Delper-managed duty-deferred bonded warehouse with automated doorstep delivery, clearing customs immediately on buyer payment.

Revenue model: A locked-in, permanent 15.184% transaction rate on core trades, plus a 6.0% net annual liquidity spread on cargo-secured facilities.

Delper Retail

Core process: A separate profit center where buyers post enquiries and pre-vetted factories bid in real time. Onboarding runs through QR codes tagged to the distributors who brought them in.

Ecosystem anchors: ONDC for buyer reach and Axis Bank for delivery-verified buyer escrow.

Revenue model: A 24.2% transaction rate ($72.67 gross profit on a baseline $300 order), made up of sales commissions and lead generation charges.
