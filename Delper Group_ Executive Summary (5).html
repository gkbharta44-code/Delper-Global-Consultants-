Dual-Track Operational Architecture

Track A: Delper Global Consultants (Singapore SPV)

  • Focus: Cross-border B2B bulk enterprise trade platform utilizing Radiant's trust-layer integration model.
  • Infrastructure Spend (USD 4.0M): Allocated immediately into SAP BTP & Signavio middleware ($0.86M), SAP S/4HANA Core ERP ($0.39M), and SGS quality webhooks ($0.15M).
  • Unit Economics: Operates on a 15.184% fixed transaction rate over a rapid 14-day settlement loop (26 capital turns per year from Year 2), plus a 6.0% net annual liquidity spread on cargo-secured facilities.

Track B: Delper Retail (India Domestic Engine)

  • Focus: India-focused, buyer-first digital marketplace natively integrated with the Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC).
  • Milestone Capital Deployment (USD 4.0M):
    • Tranche 1 ($1.0M): Immediate setup, legal licensing, and engineering team onboarding.
    • Tranche 2 ($1.5M): Triggered on execution of formal Axis Bank escrow terms and SGS 3PQC partnership terms.
    • Tranche 3 ($1.5M): Funds a comprehensive 6-to-9 month pilot phase (30–50 factories, 15–25 DTC brands).
  • Unit Economics: Generates a 24.2% e-commerce take rate ($72.67 gross profit per baseline $300 order) driven by real-time factory bidding and viral QR-distributor onboarding.

Investor Risk Mitigation: The 12-Month Exit Pipe

To completely de-risk the investor's USD 8.00 million cash commitment, Delper runs an isolated financial vehicle in parallel to the core technology tracks:

  • The Vehicle: Routes 800,000 metric tons of bulk palm oil annually through Irrevocable At-Sight Letters of Credit (LCs).
  • The Trust Pool: Capturing a locked logistics fee of USD 10 per ton builds a ring-fenced USD 8.00 Million Bare Trust pool.
  • Principal Return: 100% of the seed capital (USD 8.0M) is returned to the investor as a single lump-sum payout within 12 months. Net cash risk drops to USD 0.00. Investor exits.
